03 — WHO WE ARE

Different lives. Shared humanity.

We are people from different backgrounds, professions, experiences and walks of life. We don't have to be the same to find common ground.

Some members arrive knowing people. Others arrive knowing nobody. Some are naturally outgoing. Others take time to open up. There is space for all of them.

We want the person who has had a difficult week to feel welcome. We want the person looking for new friendships to find them. We want people to discover that connection can happen in the most ordinary moments.
